A Series of Unfortunate Events at UCSC

It’s another rainy day at UCSC, you wake up earlier than your alarm and can’t fall back asleep even though you're tired. You were up all night studying for your midterm today.

Are you ready for the day?

You only got three hours of sleep. zzzzzz....

A confident zombie.

You look in the mirror. You look like a zombie, but you tell yourself, "I got this." You definitely don't got this.

Do I really have to...?

You stare at your bed for a few seconds. You want to go back to sleep, but your midterm isn't going to take itself. Unfortunately.

You get up and get ready because you have to walk 20 minutes to your bus (Front St) as well as living in another city because Santa Cruz has a housing crisis. It never gets easier, only more exhausting. “Anything for affordable housing”, you think to yourself as you leave your apartment to catch the bus.

You leave and you walk to the bus stop, you start sprinting because it's 10 MINUTES EARLY?!, you’re sprinting and the bus driver almost drives off but you beg to let you on the bus because you’re chasing it down the main road. He lets you in but he tells you to never chase the bus again. You think it might be your lucky day since he let you on and usually those bus drivers never stop.

As you sit on the bus, your eyelids… get heavy, your jacket keeps you warm… and c…o..z..y…

You wake up in a panic, all of a sudden you’re approaching the Oakes stop with the bus full of students. Your stop was at Stevenson and your midterm started 10 minutes ago! You get up immediately and push your way out of the bus when it stops

You sprint out of the bus, no matter what your reply would’ve been to the body blocker bus riders they would’ve given you a dirty look regardless. You run up the Oakes hill and it begins down pouring, you realize you left your umbrella at home. The minute you get under the bus stop you check the weather and it is 100% rain at the next bus stop, hoping one comes soon…

“Wait result” - A bus comes and splashes you leaving you drenched when you arrive. When the bus driver opens the door she is apologizing hysterically and tells you to come in. You walk in and it’s completely full of stinky and damp underclassmen, they clown on you because you look like you just went for a swim at the UCSC pool. With the amount of social anxiety you have you decide it’s better to get off and wait for an emptier bus to spare yourself the embarrassment. You pray that a bus will come soon.

”wait, again… result”- A bus comes and you get on, luckily there are less people but you’re now being stared at weirdly again by the bus driver and other riders. You check the time and it’s been 35 minutes since your midterm started. You’re determined to make it and be able to take the testt.
